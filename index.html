Installing

  1. Download the zip and extract the whole folder.
  2. Double-click install.bat. A setup page opens in your browser and walks you through it.
  3. To update later, run the new install.bat over your old install. Your settings, counters and commands are kept, and a backup is made first.

Good to know

  • Everything runs on your computer. Nothing is sent to the developer. See the privacy notice.
  • Windows may warn about a file it does not recognise. Compare the checksum above with the one on your download if you want to be sure it is untouched.
