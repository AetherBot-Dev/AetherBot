Privacy

What AetherBot sees, what it does, and where it all goes.

Nothing leaves your computer

AetherBot sends the developer no data at all. There is no account, no tracking and no analytics. The bot, its control panel and everything it saves run on your computer, and the only place the bot talks to is Twitch.

What it sees

  • Your chat: who said what, and when people join, follow, subscribe or raid.
  • What other moderators do in your channel, if you have connected your Twitch account.

What it does

  • Answers commands and posts the messages you set up.
  • Removes messages, times out or bans people according to your settings.
  • Keeps the counters, leaderboards and logs you see in the control panel.

What it cannot see or do

  • It never sees your Twitch password. You log in on twitch.tv.
  • It cannot read whispers, other people’s accounts or any channel but yours.
  • It can only do what you allow: the bot account’s permissions, and the switches on the Twitch connection page.
  • It does nothing while it is stopped.

What it keeps, and where

  • Settings, commands, counters, the banned list and when chatters were last seen, in the bot’s data folder.
  • The mod log, which includes chat messages.
  • Your Twitch logins, in tokens.json and broadcaster-tokens.json. Treat these like passwords and do not share them.

Delete the bot’s folder and all of it is gone. Disconnect on the control panel’s Twitch connection page, or Settings → Connections on twitch.tv, takes back the bot’s access.

This website

This site is a plain page hosted on GitHub Pages and the download is hosted on GitHub Releases. AetherBot does not add any tracking to it. GitHub may keep its own ordinary server logs; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

AetherBot is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Twitch Interactive, Inc.
